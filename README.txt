S1 — final v1 local build

Files:
- index.html
- cover.png
- 01.mp3 ... 06.mp3

Open index.html in a browser.

The six track labels are temporary s1_01 ... s1_06 because the uploaded MP3 files did not contain title metadata. Replace those six labels in index.html with your actual date/time names when ready.

Player behavior:
- only one track can play at a time;
- every newly selected track starts at 0:00;
- clicking the active track stops and resets it;
- the timeline is made from "-" characters and is clickable for seeking;
- no download button is provided.
